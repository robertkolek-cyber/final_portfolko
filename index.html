My protocol

How I keep complex work moving.

01

Map the system

I start by naming actors, states, constraints, and failure paths so the interface is solving the real knot.

02

Prototype the risk

The first prototype is for the part that can break trust: setup, decision points, handoff, or re-entry.

03

Make it shippable

I turn the chosen direction into readable flows, states, specs, and feedback loops for the team.
